## How to Use These Checklists

When producing any BA deliverable, scan these checklists against the proposed change:
1. Work through each regulation's checklist
2. Mark each item as: **Applicable** (this change affects it), **Not applicable** (no impact), or **Uncertain** (needs compliance team input)
3. For applicable items, document: what's affected, what action is needed, and who owns it
4. Include the completed checklist as an appendix to the BRD or impact assessment
5. Route uncertain items to the compliance team for determination

## Solvency II

Solvency II governs capital requirements, risk management, and reporting for insurers operating in the EU/UK.

### Pillar 1 — Quantitative Requirements

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| S2-01 | Does this change affect how technical provisions are calculated? | Changes to claims reserving, premium calculations, or actuarial models | Engage actuarial team; assess SCR/MCR impact |
| S2-02 | Does this change the risk profile of the undertaking? | New product, new territory, change in underwriting criteria, reinsurance restructure | Notify risk function; update ORSA |
| S2-03 | Does this change affect asset-liability matching? | Changes to investment strategy, policy term structures, or cash flow patterns | Engage investment and actuarial teams |
| S2-04 | Does this affect reinsurance arrangements? | Changes to treaty terms, cession rules, or retrocession | Update reinsurance recoverables in SCR calculation |

### Pillar 2 — Governance & Risk Management

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| S2-05 | Does this change require updating the ORSA? | Material change in risk profile, new product launch, significant process change | Trigger ORSA refresh; notify CRO |
| S2-06 | Does this change affect the system of governance? | Changes to decision-making authorities, key functions, outsourcing | Update governance documentation; notify board |
| S2-07 | Does this involve outsourcing a critical or important function? | New third-party provider, change in outsourced service scope | Outsourcing policy review; regulatory notification may be required |
| S2-08 | Does this change affect the fit and proper requirements for key function holders? | Role changes, new key function holders | Fit and proper assessment; PRA/FCA notification |
| S2-09 | Does this change the underwriting policy? | Changes to risk appetite, acceptance criteria, pricing methodology | Board approval required; update underwriting policy document |

### Pillar 3 — Reporting & Disclosure

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| S2-10 | Does this change affect QRT (Quantitative Reporting Template) data? | Changes to data definitions, data sources, or calculation methods | Update QRT mapping; engage actuarial and finance reporting teams |
| S2-11 | Does this affect the SFCR (Solvency and Financial Condition Report)? | Material changes to risk profile, governance, capital position | Update SFCR narrative; board approval |
| S2-12 | Does this change affect RSR (Regular Supervisory Report) content? | Changes to business strategy, risk profile, governance | Update RSR; engage regulatory affairs |

## Insurance Distribution Directive (IDD)

IDD governs how insurance products are distributed — demands and needs, product governance, conflicts of interest, and transparency.

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| IDD-01 | Does this change affect the demands and needs assessment process? | Changes to sales journey, question sets, recommendation logic | Update demands and needs process; retrain distributors |
| IDD-02 | Does this change the product approval process (POG)? | New product, material product change, new distribution channel | Trigger Product Oversight and Governance review |
| IDD-03 | Does this introduce or change cross-selling practices? | Bundled products, add-ons, ancillary products | Assess whether customer benefit is demonstrated |
| IDD-04 | Does this affect how conflicts of interest are managed? | Changes to commission structures, remuneration, incentive schemes | Update conflicts of interest policy; disclose to customers |
| IDD-05 | Does this change the information provided to customers pre-sale? | Changes to product documentation, IPID, key features | Update customer-facing documents; legal review |
| IDD-06 | Does this affect the training and competence of distribution staff? | New product features, changed processes, new distribution channel | Update training materials; assess CPD requirements |
| IDD-07 | Does this change affect record-keeping of distribution activities? | Changes to CRM, application capture, advice recording | Ensure adequate records are maintained for regulatory period |

## GDPR & Data Protection

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| GDPR-01 | Does this change involve collecting new personal data? | New data fields, new data sources, new customer touchpoints | Update privacy notice; assess lawful basis; update ROPA |
| GDPR-02 | Does this change how personal data is processed? | New processing activities, AI/ML on personal data, automated decision-making | DPIA may be required; update ROPA |
| GDPR-03 | Does this involve automated decision-making with legal effects? | AI underwriting decisions, automated claims decisions, credit scoring | GDPR Art. 22 — right to human review; explainability requirement |
| GDPR-04 | Does this change data retention periods? | New data storage, changes to archival policy | Align with retention schedule; ensure deletion capability |
| GDPR-05 | Does this involve sharing personal data with third parties? | New data processors, data sharing agreements, reinsurance data flows | Data processing agreement required; assess adequacy for international transfers |
| GDPR-06 | Does this involve international data transfers? | Cloud hosting outside UK/EEA, offshore processing, global reinsurance | Adequacy decision, SCCs, or other transfer mechanism required |
| GDPR-07 | Does this change affect data subject rights? | Changes to customer portals, data access, correction, deletion capabilities | Ensure all rights are supported: access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection |
| GDPR-08 | Does this involve special category data? | Medical data, biometric data, genetic data | Explicit consent or insurance-specific lawful basis; enhanced security measures |
| GDPR-09 | Does this change require a DPIA? | Large-scale processing, new technology, systematic monitoring, special category data at scale | Conduct DPIA before processing begins; consult DPO |
| GDPR-10 | Does this affect breach notification capability? | Changes to data flows, new systems, new data stores | Ensure 72-hour notification capability; update breach response plan |

## FCA Consumer Duty

The Consumer Duty (FCA PS22/9) requires firms to deliver good outcomes for retail customers across four areas.

### Products and Services

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| CD-01 | Does this change affect the target market definition? | Product changes, distribution changes, new customer segments | Update target market assessment; manufacturer/distributor obligations |
| CD-02 | Does this change the value assessment of the product? | Pricing changes, benefit changes, claims process changes | Reassess fair value; document value assessment |
| CD-03 | Could this change cause foreseeable harm to customers? | Process changes, automation, reduced human oversight | Assess harm scenarios; implement mitigations |

### Price and Value

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| CD-04 | Does this change affect product pricing or charges? | Premium changes, fee changes, commission changes | Fair value assessment required; document rationale |
| CD-05 | Does this change affect the distribution of value between firm and customer? | Commission restructure, claims process changes, cost reduction | Demonstrate customer benefit from changes |

### Consumer Understanding

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| CD-06 | Does this change affect how products/services are communicated? | Changed documents, digital journey changes, automated communications | Test comprehension; plain language review |
| CD-07 | Does this change affect vulnerable customer handling? | Process changes, automation, channel changes | Assess impact on vulnerable customers; ensure no disadvantage |

### Consumer Support

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| CD-08 | Does this change affect how customers access support? | Channel changes, automation, self-service introduction | Ensure support is accessible, responsive, and meets diverse needs |
| CD-09 | Does this change affect the claims experience? | Claims process changes, automation, assessment criteria | Assess whether claims outcomes remain fair; monitor outcomes data |
| CD-10 | Does this change affect the complaints process? | Process changes, response time changes, resolution approach | Ensure complaints handling remains FCA-compliant |

## Lloyd's Market Standards

For London Market insurance programmes, additional standards apply.

| # | Check | Applicability trigger | Action if applicable |
|---|---|---|---|
| LM-01 | Does this change affect the Electronic Claims File (ECF)? | Claims process changes, system integration changes | Align with Lloyd's ECF standards; test with Lloyd's |
| LM-02 | Does this affect Placing Platform Limited (PPL) interactions? | Placing process changes, slip/MRC changes | Ensure PPL compliance; update broker workflows |
| LM-03 | Does this change affect syndicate reporting to Lloyd's? | Data changes, reporting frequency, new product classes | Align with Lloyd's reporting requirements |
| LM-04 | Does this change affect delegated authority arrangements? | Coverholder changes, binding authority scope, TPA arrangements | Lloyd's DA oversight requirements; crystal reporting |
| LM-05 | Does this affect Lloyd's minimum standards? | Underwriting, claims, or reinsurance process changes | Review against applicable Lloyd's minimum standards |
